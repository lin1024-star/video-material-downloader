The launcher and original application source are provided under the MIT

This package does not contain yt-dlp or FFmpeg executables. On the user's
first download, the application obtains standalone Windows components from
their respective publishers and verifies the published SHA-256 checksums.
Those components retain their own licenses and are separate programs invoked
as command-line tools. No copied third-party code is included in this GUI.
